    System Architecture & Methodology

    About This System

    A large-scale student academic performance regression system engineered with deep learning and behavioral learning analytics.

    Large-Scale OULAD Dataset

    Open University Learning Analytics Dataset: 32,593 student enrollments across 28,785 unique students, 22 presentations, and over 10.6 million clickstream interaction records.

    Deep Learning Architecture

    Tabular ResNet Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) with Batch Normalization, Dropout (0.3), SiLU nonlinear activations, residual skip connections, and AdamW weight decay optimization. Blended with tuned XGBoost and HistGradientBoosting for maximum generalization.

    Strict Leakage Prevention

    Strict student-level GroupShuffleSplit (70% train, 15% validation, 15% holdout test) guarantees that no individual student appears in multiple splits. Preprocessing pipelines are fit strictly on training splits. All input features are restricted to the first 60 days.

    Responsible & Actionable AI

    Predictions represent statistical estimates accompanied by RMSE prediction intervals. Designed exclusively to provide proactive academic advisors with early warnings to offer tutoring, study planning, and motivational support.
